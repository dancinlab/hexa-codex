# HONEST RESIDUALS (what the new fit family still does NOT capture)
# ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
# - The QUADRATIC in log2(params) is a closed-form 3-parameter family that ADMITS
#   dip-then-recover by construction (it CAN bend) — but on n=4 points with 3 free
#   parameters, the structural improvement over the monotone logistic is small
#   (ΔRMSE ≈ 0.0023, ~4% relative). The quadratic fit is statistically a low-DOF
#   reach; the load-bearing finding is the PER-AXIS stratification, NOT the
#   parametric law for OVERALL.
# - The 3B counting dip (2/5) is treated by the verifier as a PER-RUNG DUMMY — it
#   is a real measured anomaly local to the Qwen2.5-VL-3B model on the dense
#   5-9-object subitizing regime, NOT a scaling law that predicts further dips at
#   different scales. Q8_0 Qwen-VL-3B was previously probed (cycle-20) and counts
#   IDENTICALLY to Q4_K_M, ruling out quantization as the cause; the cleanest
#   interpretation is a model-specific perception-vs-counting trade Qwen-VL-3B
#   makes at that param count.
# - The 7B counting recovery (5/5) is consistent with a "subitizing capability
#   emerges at scale" hypothesis BUT a single 7B rung cannot DISCRIMINATE this
#   from "Qwen2.5-VL-7B specifically subitizes well, regardless of params". A 5th
#   rung at a non-Qwen-7B (e.g. an InternVL-7B or LLaVA-NeXT-7B with a loading
#   mmproj on llama.cpp) would discriminate the family-effect from the scale-effect.
# - The PERCEPTION sub-ladder saturates at 11/11 from rung 1 (2.2B) onwards, so
#   the 7B rung confirms saturation but cannot probe FURTHER perception capacity
#   — the manifest needs harder perception items (5+ word OCR, occluded shapes,
#   ambiguous spatial relations) to be non-saturating at the 7B end. This is a
#   manifest-extension residual, not a verifier residual.
# - The QUADRATIC fit's coefficients (a=0.01, b=0.02, c=0.84) on a 40×40×40 coarse
#   grid are not the global minimum within rounding; a finer grid or a closed-form
#   3-point least-squares solve would reach the analytical minimum. The 4-point
#   RMSE difference vs the monotone logistic is preserved either way (the quadratic
#   STRICTLY beats — by construction, since the monotone logistic cannot bend
#   downward at all).
