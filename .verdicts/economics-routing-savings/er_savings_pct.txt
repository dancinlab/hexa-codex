SUPPORTED-NUMERICAL — savings_pct derived from measured baseline + router totals.

Inputs (measured via claude --bare -p --output-format json):
  baseline.total_cost_usd = 0.299947  (always-opus × 20 tasks)
  router.total_cost_usd   = 0.081914  (length-heuristic × 20 tasks)

Closed-form derivation:
  savings_pct = (baseline.total - router.total) / baseline.total * 100
              = (0.299947 - 0.081914) / 0.299947 * 100
              = 0.218033 / 0.299947 * 100
              = 72.6906%

Reproducible via: hexa run bench/economics_routing.hexa
