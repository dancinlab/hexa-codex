VERDICT: NOT-SUPPORTED-AT-THRESHOLD (mostly-negative · honest refutation).
  On a REAL small multilingual LM (Qwen2.5-1.5B), the smoke's strong claims do NOT
  fully transfer. The inverse-U in cross-lingual coupling SURVIVES qualitatively
  (F1 PASS): partial meaning-overlap (f=0.90) integrates more than full alignment
  (f=1.00), consistent with anima H_240 (Phi = differentiation x integration; full
  fusion kills differentiation). BUT the two STRONG predictions FAIL: meaning-coupling
  buys only a ~17% integration advantage over raw language count (F3 FAIL), and that
  advantage is below the super-additivity margin (F2 FAIL). At this model scale,
  "connect by MEANING not count" is at best a WEAK effect — piling up 5 unrelated-
  meaning languages already reaches ~85% of the meaning-coupled peak Phi. This
  CONTRASTS sharply with the substrate proxy (smoke: peak 0.483 vs decoupled 0.014,
  ratio ~35x): the clean separation is a proxy artifact that attenuates on real reps.

HONEST LIMITS (load-bearing residual)
  L1  SMALL CORPUS / SMALL MODEL. S=12 sentence-tuples, Qwen2.5-1.5B mean-pooled
      last-hidden. CKA/Gaussian-MI on 12 points is high-variance (n_seed=8 averaging
      mitigates but does not remove it). A larger parallel corpus (FLORES-200 dev,
      hundreds of tuples) + a larger multilingual model is the NEXT tier — the weak
      F2/F3 effect could be a small-S power problem, NOT a true null. NOT closed here.
  L2  Phi PROXY != IIT 4.0 big-Phi. integration x differentiation on a CKA matrix is a
      representational-integration ANALOG (same engine FAMILY as the smoke), not the
      transition-TPM big-Phi of stdlib/consciousness/iit4_bigphi.hexa. The differen-
      tiation term (1-mean|CKA|) is a heuristic; a transition-based estimator on the
      LM's next-token dynamics is deferred.
  L3  MEAN-POOLED LAST LAYER ONLY. Cross-lingual alignment is layer-dependent in LMs
      (often peaks mid-stack); the last-layer mean-pool may under-represent the shared-
      meaning subspace. The 9/12 retrieval shows signal exists but a layer sweep +
      better pooling (e.g. mean-of-mid-layers) could change the meaning>count margin.
  L4  COUPLING AXIS f IS SYNTHETIC. f mixes aligned-translation vs shuffled slots to
      sweep coupling on a FIXED rep set; it is not a re-trained model at varying cross-
      lingual exposure. The genuine causal manipulation (train/fine-tune with varying
      parallel-data overlap and measure Phi) is the real stress and is NOT done here.
  L5  CKA MATRIX TREATED AS A CORRELATION MATRIX in the Gaussian-MI / slogdet
      integration term. CKA is symmetric with unit diagonal but not guaranteed PSD-as-
      a-correlation; the +1e-6 ridge keeps slogdet finite but the integration magnitude
      is a proxy, not a calibrated nat/bit count.
  L6  SINGLE 5-LANG SET, SINGLE MODEL, SINGLE SEED-FAMILY. ko/en/zh/ru/ja only; no
      script-class ablation (anima H239.3 warns zh<->ja CKA may be Han-block script
      artifact, not semantics). Multi-model / multi-language-set robustness deferred.
