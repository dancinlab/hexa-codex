\begin{abstract}
Per the hexa-codex \texttt{cx\_paper\_gate} workflow rule, a paper ships only
when every section claim reaches a 100\% formal-blue or numerical-green verdict.
We report the OPS-domain finding measured on a self-hosted M3 Metal
\texttt{llama-server} (Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct-Q4\_K\_M, Homebrew
\texttt{llama.cpp}): an 18-cell offered-load grid (3 parallel-slot counts
\(\texttt{-np}\in\{1,2,4\}\) \(\times\) 6 arrival rates
\(\{1,2,5,10,20,40\}\) qps) over the Stage-2 \(N{=}2000\) manifest, yielding a
full p50/p95/p99(/p999) \(\times\) accuracy service surface (12 \texttt{VALID}
\(+\) 6 \texttt{WALL\_CAPPED} honest partials, 0 boot-fail, 0 hang). The surface
is reproduced by a closed-form M/M/c (Erlang-C) queueing model: treating each
\(\texttt{-np}\) slot as a service channel \(c\), the saturation throughput
equals the \(c\cdot\mu\) ceiling (measured \(9.53/15.01/20.0\) qps for
\(c{=}1/2/4\)), the latency knee shifts \emph{right} with \(c\) (overturning the
coarse-grid pilot's \(\texttt{best\_np}{=}1\)), and offered load beyond the
ceiling buys zero goodput (\(\lambda < c\cdot\mu\)). The most operationally
load-bearing result is on the \emph{correctness} axis: a saturation SLO
violation surfaces as an \emph{accuracy} cliff through two separable mechanisms
--- client-timeout truncation and continuous-batch scheduler slot-preemption ---
so a latency-only dashboard misses both. \textbf{Scope (not claimed):} the
\emph{absolute} knee utilization is host-load-sensitive (the harness's own
per-request shell pipeline competes for the shared UMA) and is therefore
explicitly excluded; only the scale-invariant M/M/c structure is certified.
The \S Formula recompute (\texttt{verify/numerics\_ops\_mmc\_knee.hexa}) passes
5/5 checks; verdict \texttt{.verdicts/sandbox/m4\_ops\_formula\_fit.txt}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Formula}
\label{sec:formula}
\textcolor{green!50!black}{\textbf{SUPPORTED-NUMERICAL}} --- the measured serving
surface is the classical \(M/M/c\) (Erlang-C) multi-server queue
\citep{erlang1917,kleinrock1975}. Each \texttt{llama.cpp} continuous-batch slot
(\(\texttt{-np}\)) is a service channel; with \(c\) channels, per-channel service
rate \(\mu\) and Poisson arrival rate \(\lambda\),
\begin{align}
  a &= \lambda/\mu, \qquad \rho = a/c = \lambda/(c\,\mu), \\
  C(c,a) &= \frac{a^{c}/\!\big(c!\,(1-\rho)\big)}
               {\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{c-1}\frac{a^{k}}{k!}
                \;+\; \frac{a^{c}}{c!\,(1-\rho)}}, \\
  W_q &= \frac{C(c,a)}{c\,\mu - \lambda}, \qquad
  W = W_q + \frac{1}{\mu},
\end{align}
where \(C(c,a)\) is the Erlang-C probability that an arrival must queue and \(W\)
is the mean sojourn time. Two closed-form consequences govern the SLO surface:
the \emph{throughput ceiling} \(\lambda_{\max} = c\,\mu\) (no goodput beyond it,
the stability bound \(\lambda < c\,\mu\)) and the \emph{latency knee}, where
\(W\to\infty\) as the utilization \(\rho\to 1\) (a pole, not a smooth bend).

\paragraph{Fit and recompute.} The effective per-slot service rate is read from
the measured saturation throughput, \(\mu_{\mathrm{eff}}(c) = \text{ceiling}/c\)
--- \emph{not} assumed constant: shared-UMA memory bandwidth makes it degrade
with \(c\) (\(9.53 \to 7.505 \to 5.0\) req/s/slot for \(c{=}1/2/4\)). The
verifier \texttt{verify/numerics\_ops\_mmc\_knee.hexa} recomputes the law from
\(\{c,\mu_{\mathrm{eff}}\}\) and the measured grid and checks five invariants
(all pass): (1) the ceiling identity \(c\,\mu_{\mathrm{eff}} =\) measured
throughput (error \(0.0\) qps); (2) the ceiling grows monotonically with \(c\)
(\(9.53 < 15.01 < 20.0\) qps) so the knee shifts \emph{right}; (3) the highest
in-SLO sustained rate (\(\{2,10,20\}\) qps for \(c{=}1/2/4\)) is strictly
increasing in \(c\) and every operating point sits at \(\rho \le 1\); (4) the
stability cap holds --- throughput at the highest offered rate (40 qps) never
exceeds \(c\,\mu\); (5) the Erlang-C sojourn is a pole --- \(W\) is strictly
increasing in \(\lambda\), rises \(7.7\times\) over \(\rho{=}0.5\to0.95\), and
diverges at \(\rho{=}1\).

\paragraph{Scope (explicitly not claimed).} The \emph{absolute} knee
utilization is \emph{not} asserted. The measured per-request latency includes
the bench harness's own shell-pipeline tax (per-request \texttt{xargs}/\texttt{jq}/\texttt{awk}),
exogenous to the \(M/M/c\) service process, which depresses the single-slot
service rate in this run (\(\mu_{c=1}\approx 3.4\)/s) below its transient
ceiling (\(9.53\)/s) and shifts the apparent knee earlier for \(c{=}2,4\). Only
the \emph{scale-invariant} structure --- ceiling \(=c\,\mu\), knee shifts right,
stability cap, Erlang-C pole --- is certified; verdict
\texttt{.verdicts/sandbox/m4\_ops\_formula\_fit.txt} (5/5).

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
\textcolor{green!50!black}{\textbf{SUPPORTED-NUMERICAL}} --- the SLO harness is
\texttt{bench/sandbox\_stage4\_slo\_full\_grid.hexa}, run cycle-16 on a
self-hosted M3 Metal mac mini (24\,GB UMA) serving
\texttt{Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct-Q4\_K\_M} \citep{qwen25} through Homebrew
\texttt{llama.cpp} \citep{llamacpp} on port 8090. For each
\((\texttt{-np}, \text{rate})\) cell it boots a fresh \texttt{llama-server} at
the slot count, drives a Poisson-spaced open-loop arrival stream at the target
qps against the Stage-2 \(N{=}2000\) manifest, and records per-request wall
latency and \texttt{byte\_exact\_subset} correctness. Pre-registered protocol:
\SI{15}{\second} warmup (excluded from the percentile rollup),
\SI{60}{\second} measure window with a 2000-request budget, \(p999\) reported
only when \(n_{\text{completed}}\ge 1000\), per-request timeout \SI{30}{\second},
and a \SI{240}{\second} per-cell wall cap (cells hitting it report
\texttt{WALL\_CAPPED} percentiles over the partial drain --- an honest partial,
never a hang). Two harness hardening fixes vs the M2.OPS pilot: a wait-for-port-free
poll \(+\) 3-retry boot (\textbf{FIX-R1}, eliminating the port-teardown race ---
0/18 boot-fails vs 2/9 in the pilot) and the \SI{240}{\second} wall cap
(\textbf{FIX-R2}, replacing the pilot's \(\sim\)30-min hang). \(\texttt{-np}{=}8\)
is excluded \emph{a priori}: cycle-6 measured it 27.9\% slower than
\(\texttt{-np}{=}4\) (16\,GB-UMA memory-bandwidth saturation). The scorer is
\texttt{byte\_exact\_subset} only (no LLM self-judge, per the SANDBOX honesty
rule); transport errors (\texttt{error\_rate}, non-2xx) and content correctness
(\texttt{accuracy}) are kept strictly separate. All cells ran at \$0 incremental
on local Metal. Verdict header:
\texttt{.verdicts/sandbox/m3\_ops\_full\_slo\_grid\_summary.txt}.

\section{Benefit}
\label{sec:benefit}
\textcolor{green!50!black}{\textbf{SUPPORTED-NUMERICAL}} --- the grid lands three
paper-grade, deployment-actionable deltas:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The knee shifts right with \(\texttt{-np}\) --- best-\texttt{np}
    guidance (overturns the pilot).} At \texttt{rate=5}, \(\texttt{-np}{=}2\)
    serves at p50 \SI{266}{\milli\second} where \(\texttt{-np}{=}1\) has already
    saturated (p50 \SI{4400}{\milli\second}) --- a \(16.5\times\) latency gap;
    \(\texttt{-np}{=}2\) sustains \texttt{rate=10} cleanly (VALID, full 600-req
    budget, acc 94\%) and \(\texttt{-np}{=}4\) sustains \texttt{rate=20} at p50
    \SI{1778}{\milli\second} on the full 1200-req budget. The M2.OPS pilot's
    \(\texttt{best\_np}{=}1\) was a coarse-grid artifact (its \(\{5,20,100\}\)
    grid never sampled the 5--10 qps band where extra slots win). The actionable
    rule: provision \(c\) channels to the \emph{right} of the target offered
    rate, since the sustainable rate scales roughly with \(c\).
  \item \textbf{A latency SLO violation is an \emph{accuracy} cliff --- via two
    distinct mechanisms.} (a) \emph{Client-timeout truncation}: when the long
    tail exceeds the request timeout, truncated/empty bodies score
    byte-exact-WRONG. (b) \emph{Scheduler slot-preemption} (new, \(c\ge 2\)):
    the continuous-batch scheduler preempts in-flight generations to admit new
    arrivals, returning short-but-HTTP-200 content that fails the scorer; this
    is why \(\texttt{-np}\ge 2\) accuracy collapses at \texttt{rate}\(\ge 20\)
    \emph{despite} p99 staying well under the timeout and \texttt{error\_rate}
    \(=0.00\%\). \textbf{A latency-only SLO dashboard misses both} --- the
    correctness axis is mandatory in any LLM-serving SLO.
  \item \textbf{Throughput is hard-capped at \(c\,\mu\); over-provisioning
    offered rate is pure waste.} Pushing offered rate to 40 qps never raises
    goodput above \(9.53/15.01/20.0\) qps (the measured ceilings); it buys only
    queue depth (rising p50/p99) and, past the knee, the accuracy cliff above.
    Capacity planning should target \(\rho < 1\) against \(c\,\mu\), not the
    offered-rate headline.
\end{itemize}

All three benefits are measured at \$0 incremental cost on self-hosted M3 Metal;
the harness itself --- which closes all three M2.OPS residuals (boot-race,
saturation-hang, unlocated knee) --- is part of the contribution.

\section{Limitations and honest caveats}
\label{sec:limits}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Absolute knee utilization is host-load-sensitive and not
    claimed.} The bench harness's own per-request shell pipeline competes for
    the shared UMA, depressing the single-slot service rate in this run
    (\(\mu_{c=1}\approx 3.4\)/s vs the pilot's \(\approx 8.9\)/s). Only the
    scale-invariant M/M/c structure is certified; a deployment with a different
    box, model, or arrival driver will see a different \emph{absolute} knee qps.
  \item \textbf{Single model, single box.} 0.5B Q4 on one 24\,GB-UMA M3; the
    \(\mu_{\mathrm{eff}}\)-degrades-with-\(c\) (memory-bandwidth) effect is
    specific to shared-memory Apple Silicon and will differ on discrete-GPU
    hosts with per-stream bandwidth.
  \item \textbf{WALL\_CAPPED cells are honest partials.} Six cells report
    percentiles over their \SI{240}{\second} partial drain; their p50/p99 are
    valid samples of the saturated regime, but \(n_{\text{completed}}\) is an
    undercount of true demand.
  \item \textbf{Publish-lint (g51) residual.} This 4-section draft does not yet
    meet the commons \(\ge 10\)-page \(+\) fal.ai-figure publish bar; that is a
    separate \textbf{M5.OPS-release} condition, deliberately not padded here.
\end{itemize}
