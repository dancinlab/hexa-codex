\begin{abstract}
Two popular ``consciousness-by-proxy'' hypotheses claim that integrated
information $\Phi$ is (i) \emph{caused} by recurrence and (ii) a marker of
genuine \emph{emergence}. We test both on real substrates with one common
$\Phi$-proxy engine and pre-registered, deterministic falsifiers. \textbf{Front~1
(cause).} On a frozen \texttt{Qwen2.5-1.5B}, we inject a GRU adapter into the
layer-14 residual stream and train it on a language-model objective (base
frozen). Recurrence does \emph{not} raise $\Phi$ above a parameter-matched
feedforward (FF) control: the FF attains strictly higher $\Phi$ on every seed
(REC band $[2.49,2.65]$ vs FF band $[2.70,2.82]$ bits, $\Delta\Phi=-0.166$),
higher self-prediction, and lower perplexity --- so capacity, not recurrence,
carries the integration. \textbf{Front~2 (consequence).} On a
leaky-integrate-and-fire ring swept order$\to$edge$\to$chaos, the edge-of-chaos
$\Phi$ peak is micro-reducible: causal emergence $\mathrm{CE}=\mathrm{EI}_{\text{macro}}-\mathrm{EI}_{\text{micro}}$
is negative at the edge under both an observational proxy ($\mathrm{CE}=-0.877$,
3 coarse-grainings) and Hoel \emph{interventional} effective information with a
grouping search ($\mathrm{CE}=-0.587$, 6 coarse-grainings) --- nine
coarse-grainings, all refuted. Integration thus dissociates from both its
hypothesized cause and its hypothesized consequence. \textbf{Scope.} $\Phi$- and
CE-proxies are necessary-condition correlates, not phenomenal consciousness; we
make no claim about subjective experience. All results recompute bit-for-bit
from the \texttt{companion/} ledger and the \texttt{LAB/} verdict files.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Integrated Information Theory (IIT) proposes $\Phi$, the information generated by
a system above and beyond its parts, as a measure tied to consciousness
\citep{tononi2016iit,oizumi2014iit3,albantakis2023iit4}. Two auxiliary claims
travel with it in practice. First, a \emph{causal} claim inherited from
recurrent-processing accounts \citep{lamme2006}: that feedback / recurrence is
what \emph{produces} high $\Phi$, so adding recurrence to a feedforward system
should raise it. Second, a \emph{consequence} claim borrowed from
causal-emergence theory \citep{hoel2013,hoel2017}: that a high-$\Phi$ regime ---
canonically the edge of chaos \citep{langton1990,beggs2003} --- is a site of
genuine emergence, where a coarse macro description has \emph{more} causal power
than the micro.

We stress-test both on real substrates. A prior synthetic smoke (a rule-110
ring) reproduced the textbook recurrence-raises-$\Phi$ pattern; here we ask
whether either claim survives contact with (a) a real trained transformer and
(b) an interventional emergence measure. They do not.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0.3em
\item A residual-stream LM-objective protocol (Front~1) showing that recurrence
  does \emph{not} cause integrated information on a real LLM: a parameter-matched
  feedforward control attains strictly higher $\Phi$, self-prediction, and lower
  perplexity, with non-overlapping seed bands.
\item A causal-emergence test (Front~2) --- observational, then Hoel
  interventional with a grouping search --- showing the edge-of-chaos $\Phi$ peak
  is micro-reducible across nine coarse-grainings.
\item Two dissociations: integration $\not\leftarrow$ recurrence (cause) and
  integration $\neq$ emergence (consequence), each with deterministic recompute.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Background}
\label{sec:bg}

The $\Phi$-proxy used throughout is the whole-minus-best-bipartition effective
information of a binary state series, a deterministic relative of IIT's
integration term \citep{oizumi2014iit3,albantakis2023iit4} and of transfer
entropy \citep{schreiber2000}. Recurrence is realized by a GRU cell
\citep{cho2014} contrasted against a parameter-matched feedforward stack; the
host LLM is a decoder-only transformer \citep{vaswani2017,qwen2025}. Causal
emergence is computed in Hoel's sense \citep{hoel2013,hoel2017}: effective
information under a maximum-entropy (uniform) intervention, compared between the
micro state space and a coarse-grained macro space.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{$\Phi$-proxy (formula).} For a binary state series $S_t$ over $K$
median-split channels,
\[
  \Phi \;=\; I(S_t;S_{t+1}) \;-\; \min_{A\,\sqcup\,B = S}\big[\,I(A_t;A_{t+1}) + I(B_t;B_{t+1})\,\big],
\]
the whole effective information minus its best (minimum-information) bipartition;
$\mathrm{self\_pred}=I(S_t;S_{t+1})$. The identical engine is used in both
fronts (Front~2 sets $S$ = spike vector).

\paragraph{Front~1 --- does recurrence cause $\Phi$?} Base
\texttt{Qwen2.5-1.5B} (28 layers, hidden 1536), float32, \emph{frozen}
($\texttt{requires\_grad}=\text{False}$). A bottleneck adapter (width 16) is
injected into the layer-14 residual stream by a forward hook,
$h \leftarrow h + \mathrm{Adapter}(h)$, LoRA-style with the up-projection
zero-initialized so step~0 equals the frozen base. \textbf{REC} uses a
\texttt{GRUCell} (recurrence over the token axis); \textbf{FF} uses a
parameter-matched 3-linear stack (52{,}336 vs 51{,}520 params, $\sim 1.6\%$ apart,
favoring REC). Both are trained only on the next-token cross-entropy of an
8-sentence corpus (Adam, lr $0.01$, 80 steps, seeds $\{42,43,44\}$); gradients
flow through the frozen upper layers. We then measure $\Phi$, self-prediction,
and perplexity on the adapter-modified layer-14 residual. \emph{Pre-registered
falsifiers:} \textbf{F2L4} $\Phi_{\text{REC}} > \Phi_{\text{FF,band-max}}$;
\textbf{F4} both adapters lower ppl vs base; \textbf{F5}
$\mathrm{self}_{\text{REC}} > \mathrm{self}_{\text{FF}}$. Headline:
$\text{supported}=\text{F2L4}$.

\paragraph{Front~2 --- is the $\Phi$-peak emergent?} An $N{=}6$
leaky-integrate-and-fire ring is swept across regimes R1 (weak/silent), R2
(noise edge), R3 (tonic edge), R4 (over-driven), the same sweep that yields the
$\Phi$ inverse-U on neuromorphic silicon (LAB-10). Causal emergence
$\mathrm{CE}=\mathrm{EI}_{\text{macro}}-\mathrm{EI}_{\text{micro}}$, with the
micro state the 6 spike units and the macro a spatial coarse-graining (pairs or
triples). \emph{Tier 2a (observational):} $\mathrm{EI}=I(S_t;S_{t+1})$ on natural
trajectories, three coarse-grainings (OR/AND/XOR). \emph{Tier 2b
(interventional, Hoel):} the transition matrix is built by $\mathrm{do}(s)$ ---
force each of the $2^6$ states, one memoryless step, sample over noise --- and
$\mathrm{EI}$ is the mutual information under a \emph{uniform} intervention;
$\mathrm{EI}_{\text{macro}}$ is the \emph{maximum} over six coarse-grainings
(pairs OR/AND/XOR, triples majority/OR/AND). \emph{Pre-registered falsifiers:}
\textbf{F1} $\mathrm{CE}(\text{edge}) > 0$; \textbf{F2} $\mathrm{CE}(\text{edge}) > \mathrm{CE}(\text{R1})$;
\textbf{F3} $\mathrm{CE}(\text{edge}) > \mathrm{CE}(\text{R4})$. All thresholds
fixed before the runs.

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\paragraph{Front~1: recurrence does not cause $\Phi$.} Table~\ref{tab:front1}.
The parameter-matched feedforward control attains strictly higher $\Phi$ than the
recurrent GRU on \emph{every} seed (REC max $2.6531 <$ FF min $2.7047$:
non-overlapping bands), higher self-prediction, and lower perplexity. Both
adapters learn the task (ppl $34.1\to{\sim}1.1$--$1.6$), so the comparison is
fair. F2L4 and F5 fail; only F4 (both-learned) passes. The stress-tier's lone
positive --- recurrence raising self-prediction --- \emph{reverses} here.

\paragraph{The two dissociations.} High integration coincides with neither its
hypothesized cause nor its hypothesized consequence: $\Phi$ is not raised by
recurrence (Front~1, FF $>$ REC) and the $\Phi$ peak is not a site of macro
causal emergence (Front~2, $\mathrm{CE}<0$). The edge of chaos is a richly
integrated \emph{micro} process.

\section{Limitations and honest caveats}
\label{sec:limits}

\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.2em
\item \textbf{Memorization regime (Front~1).} ppl ${\sim}1.1$--$1.6$ on 8
  sentences means both adapters largely memorize; the REC-vs-FF $\Phi$ comparison
  is still fair (both overfit equally, FF still higher), but absolute values are
  corpus-specific. A held-out train/val split is the next tier.
\item \textbf{Proxy, not full IIT.} $\Phi$ here is whole$-$min-bipartition over
  $K{=}6$ median-split channels, not IIT~4.0 big-$\Phi$ with purview / cause-effect
  structure search.
\item \textbf{Memoryless closure (Front~2b).} The interventional TPM resets the
  membrane each step; with these regime parameters R1/R2/R4 become sub-threshold
  ($\mathrm{EI}=0$), so the decisive test is F1, which fails large. The leaky
  observational tier (where R2 is active) agrees in sign.
\item \textbf{Grouping search is six hand-specified spatial coarse-grainings},
  not the full $64\to k$ partition space; a positive CE under some exotic
  partition is not strictly excluded, but the loss is large ($\sim$half).
\item \textbf{Sim, not silicon} (Front~2): the live AKD1000 emergence run stays
  \tierOrange.
\item \textbf{Strong (causal) emergence, not phenomenal consciousness.} A
  negative says the signature is micro-reducible at this measure; it is silent on
  subjective experience, which these proxies cannot reach.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

On real substrates, integrated information $\Phi$ comes apart from the two
properties usually attributed to it: recurrence does not cause it (a
capacity-matched feedforward integrates as well or better), and an edge-of-chaos
$\Phi$ peak is not causal emergence (it is micro-reducible across nine
coarse-grainings and an interventional measure). The detection of a $\Phi$
signature is therefore not, by itself, evidence of a recurrence mechanism or of
``the whole exceeding its parts.'' The single most important follow-up is the
substrate-grounded tier: a live AKD1000 interventional-emergence run with a full
coarse-graining search.
