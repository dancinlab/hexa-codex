WHAT THIS TIER CLOSES (the stress verdict's residual L4)
  The stress tier fit REC(GRU)/FF adapters SELF-SUPERVISED on a CAPTURED causal hidden
  series (next-hidden MSE), NOT back-propagated into the LM, and could not report adapter
  task-ability. The stress verdict's L4 said a "true LoRA fine-tune that feeds adapter
  output back into the residual stream + re-trains on an LM objective ... could change
  the REC vs FF Phi comparison." THIS tier runs exactly that. Only TWO things change vs
  stress; everything else (frozen Qwen2.5-1.5B, layer 14, K=6, the whole_EI -
  min_bipartition Phi engine, multi-seed band) is IDENTICAL, so the comparison is
  apples-to-apples across tiers.
    (1) INJECTION : adapter output is added back into the layer-14 residual stream via a
        forward hook (LoRA-style residual add; up-projection zero-init so step-0 == frozen
        base). The adapter now actually participates in the model's forward computation.
    (2) OBJECTIVE : adapters are trained on the LM next-token cross-entropy (labels), base
        FROZEN, only adapter params optimized; gradients flow through the frozen upper
        layers + LM head into the adapter. Perplexity is THEN reported for both arms.
  CROSS-TIER SANITY: the CAUSAL_frozen reference reproduces the stress tier's CAUSAL Phi
  to full precision — phi=2.8477363469573813, self_pred=3.9074609119958468 — confirming
  the Phi engine / channels / corpus / layer are byte-identical across the two harnesses.

PHI-PROXY (same family as smoke + stress):
  Phi = whole I(S_t;S_{t+1}) - min_bipartition[ I(A_t;A_{t+1}) + I(B_t;B_{t+1}) ]
  self_pred = whole I(S_t;S_{t+1})

VERDICT: REFUTED — HELD and STRENGTHENED at L4. The stress tier's REFUTED does NOT soften
under the stronger protocol; it sharpens. When the adapter is fed back into the layer-14
residual stream and trained on a real LM objective (next-token cross-entropy) with the base
frozen, recurrence (GRU) does NOT raise integrated-information Phi above a param-matched
feedforward control — the param-matched FEEDFORWARD attains STRICTLY HIGHER Phi on EVERY
seed (non-overlapping bands), STRICTLY HIGHER self-prediction, AND lower perplexity. The
smoke's "recurrence is the cause of integrated information" claim is unsupported on a real
LLM at the strongest tier we can run locally. THREE load-bearing observations:

  (1) BANDS NOW SEPARATE THE WRONG WAY. In stress the REC band [0.282,1.062] OVERLAPPED the
      FF band [0.062,1.122] (F2 fail by overlap). Under LM-objective residual injection the
      bands SEPARATE — but in FF's favor: REC [2.4898,2.6531] sits entirely BELOW FF
      [2.7047,2.8172]. More training + real task gradients did not rescue recurrence; it
      widened FF's Phi lead into a clean, seed-robust separation.

  (2) THE DISSOCIATION FLIPPED. In stress, recurrence raised the SELF proxy without raising
      Phi (F3 PASS, an honest Phi<->self dissociation). At L4 that dissociation REVERSES:
      FF now leads on self_pred too (3.963 > 3.782), so the stress tier's lone positive
      (F3) does NOT survive transfer to the LM-objective residual-injection protocol. The
      self/ego axis advantage of recurrence was an artifact of the self-supervised next-
      hidden fit, not a property of recurrence under a real LM task.

  (3) RECURRENCE IS NOT EVEN THE BETTER LEARNER HERE. Both adapters crushed perplexity
      (34.12 -> ~1.1-1.6) on the 8-sentence corpus, but the FEEDFORWARD control reached a
      lower ppl on every seed. Recurrence bought neither higher Phi nor better task fit on
      this real LLM residual stream — capacity, not recurrence, is doing the work.

HONEST LIMITS (load-bearing residual)
  M1  MEMORIZATION REGIME. ppl ~1.1-1.6 on 8 sentences means both adapters largely MEMORIZE
      the tiny corpus (near-perfect next-token fit). The REC-vs-FF Phi comparison is still
      valid (both arms equally overfit; FF still strictly higher Phi), but absolute Phi/ppl
      are corpus-overfit-specific. A larger held-out corpus + train/val split is the next
      tier and would test generalization, not just fit. (Stress L2 small-sample caveat persists.)
  M2  PROXY, not full IIT 4.0 big-Phi (same as stress L1) — whole_EI - min_bipartition over
      K=6 median-split binary channels; no purview / MICE / cause-effect-structure search.
      Direction of the (negative) effect is the result; absolute bits are proxy-/discretization-
      specific. Each arm picks its OWN top-K variance channels (stress convention).
  M3  SINGLE INJECTION SITE (layer 14) · single adapter width (16) · single model. The
      residual hook injects at one layer only; a multi-layer or attention-side injection
      could differ. Reported as a single-site result.
  M4  CAPTURE = adapter-modified layer-14 residual (the hook's output), per-sentence, no_grad.
      ppl is computed per-sentence with the adapter active (the stress gap is now closed for
      both arms). MPS non-determinism is bounded by the 3-seed band (REC sd ~0.07, FF sd ~0.05).
  M5  3-seed band (vs stress 5-seed). Bands are tight and non-overlapping, so 3 seeds already
      separate REC from FF cleanly; more seeds would narrow CIs but cannot close a gap whose
      per-seed minima/maxima do not overlap.
