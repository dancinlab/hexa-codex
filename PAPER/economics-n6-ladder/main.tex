\begin{abstract}
We pre-register and verify $11$ claims about the hexa-codex ECONOMICS
group's $n=6$ ladder via the \texttt{hexa verify} CLI. Four atlas-atom
claims ($\sigma(6),\ \varphi(6),\ \tau(6),\ \sigma_2(6)$) recompute
exactly to atlas values (\textbf{$\mathbf{4 \times}$ \textsc{Supported-Formal}}).
Four closed-form verifier suites
(\texttt{numerics\_economics\_\{cross\_pillar, scaling\_laws, pareto\}.hexa}
and \texttt{report\_economics\_ladder.hexa}) each close $10/10$ checks
(\textbf{$\mathbf{4 \times}$ \textsc{Pass}}). Three modelling-level claims
(``$\alpha = 1/6$ \emph{is} the quality\_scale exponent'';
``$\mathrm{N6\_EXP} = 24/25$ \emph{is} the train\_cost exponent'';
``Pareto $D/N \approx 1.07$ at $n=6$ vs $\approx 20$ for
Chinchilla''~\cite{hoffmann2022chinchilla}) are explicitly fenced as
\textbf{$\mathbf{3 \times}$ \textsc{Speculation-Fenced}} --- they are
internal-arithmetic-consistent but not derivable from the atlas alone.
The gate (\texttt{cx\_paper\_gate}) opens at $0$ \textsc{Falsified} +
$8$ (\textsc{Supported-Formal}+\textsc{Pass}) $\ge 1$ + all $3$
\textsc{Fenced} explicit, so this paper exists.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Closed-form scaling laws for AI economics --- training compute
$\propto N \cdot D$ \cite{hoffmann2022chinchilla,kaplan2020scaling},
inference cost as a function of context length, and the quality
returned per unit of either --- are widely cited yet rarely re-derived
algebraically from a single shared algebraic substrate. The
hexa-codex ECONOMICS group ships three verbs (\texttt{train\_cost},
\texttt{infer\_cost}, \texttt{quality\_scale}) on the $n=6$ arithmetic
lattice, with exponents $\mathrm{N6\_EXP} = 24/25$, $\tau = 4$, and
$\alpha = \varphi(6)/\sigma(6) = 1/6$ respectively.

This paper is the verification artefact for that group. Every claim
either (a) recomputes via \texttt{hexa verify --expr} from an atlas
atom and is \textsc{Supported-Formal}, (b) closes a $10/10$
\texttt{numerics\_*.hexa} sentinel via \texttt{hexa run} and is
\textsc{Pass}, or (c) is an explicit \texttt{hexa verify --fence}
modelling claim and is \textsc{Speculation-Fenced}. There is no
$\textsc{Falsified}$ or unfenced speculation in scope.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0.3em
\item A reproducible CLI-driven verification artefact for the
      ECONOMICS group's $n=6$ ladder, persisted as
      \texttt{CLAIMS.tape} + \texttt{.verdicts/economics-n6-ladder/}.
\item Tier-segregated sections (\S\ref{sec:verified},
      \S\ref{sec:pending}, \S\ref{sec:fenced}) that never merge

      $n=6$ lattice \emph{predicts} the empirical quality, training,
      or inference economics of real LLMs is fenced
      (\S\ref{sec:fenced}); \emph{only} the closed-form internal
      consistency is \textsc{Supported-Formal}/\textsc{Pass}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Background}
\label{sec:bg}

The $n=6$ lattice closure $\sigma(6) \cdot \varphi(6) = n \cdot \tau(6)
= J_2 = 24$ \cite{hoffmann2022chinchilla,kaplan2020scaling} is the
algebraic substrate underlying every ECONOMICS exponent. The
\texttt{hexa verify} CLI \cite{hexacodex2026} is the project's
single source of truth for correctness claims; verdicts are reported
on the six-tier rubric \textsc{Supported-Formal}~\textcolor{blue!60!black}{($\bullet$)} /
\textsc{Supported-Numerical}~\textcolor{green!50!black}{($\bullet$)} /
\textsc{Supported-By-Citation}~\textcolor{yellow!60!black}{($\bullet$)} /
\textsc{Insufficient/Deferred}~\textcolor{orange!80!black}{($\bullet$)} /
\textsc{Falsified}~\textcolor{red!70!black}{($\bullet$)} /
\textsc{Speculation-Fenced}~($\circ$).

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

Eleven claims were enumerated in \texttt{CLAIMS.tape} (one per row,
each with a \texttt{method} field $\in \{$\texttt{atom}, \texttt{fence},
\texttt{run}$\}$). Each was executed via the corresponding
\texttt{hexa verify} or \texttt{hexa run} invocation; raw stdout was
persisted verbatim to \texttt{.verdicts/economics-n6-ladder/<id>.txt}.
The index \texttt{.verdicts/economics-n6-ladder.tape} records
$(id, tier, raw\text{-}path)$ per claim. The gate
\texttt{cx\_paper\_gate} requires $0$~\textsc{Falsified}, $\ge 1$
\textsc{Supported}/\textsc{Pass}, and all \textsc{Fenced} entries
explicit. No claim was post-hoc downgraded or paraphrased.

\section{Results --- \textsc{Pending} tier}
\label{sec:pending}

\emph{None for this slug.} The eleven enumerated claims partition
exactly into \textsc{Supported-Formal} ($\times 4$), \textsc{Pass}
($\times 4$), and \textsc{Speculation-Fenced} ($\times 3$).
Per-verb empirical T4 contact (live HumanEval+/hexa-eval pass-rate vs
model and data scale; live latency / KV-cache profiles; live
HumanEval+) is the deferred empirical layer for the ECONOMICS group;
it lives in the project release ladder (v1.2.0+) and is intentionally
out of scope for this artefact, per \texttt{cx\_claim\_verify}.

\section{Results --- \textsc{Speculation-Fenced} tier}

\texttt{hexa verify --fence}. Closed-form arithmetic from the four

\section{Limitations and honest caveats}
\label{sec:limits}

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0.3em
\item The \textsc{Pass} tier verifies that each
      \texttt{verify/numerics\_*.hexa} script closes its internal
      $10/10$ check, not that the underlying scaling law fits any
      external benchmark. T4 empirical contact is \textsc{Pending}.
\item Several internal identities in the \textsc{Pass} tier (e.g.\
      Lagrangian optimum $(N/D)^\alpha = A/B$) are
      tautological-by-construction: the closed-form satisfies the
      identity because the function definition encodes it.
\item The \textsc{Speculation-Fenced} tier is the load-bearing
      epistemic gap: arithmetic from atlas atoms is exact, but
      identifying that arithmetic with empirical AI economics is a
      modelling assumption.
\item The \texttt{verify/run\_all.hexa} orchestrator was not run
      end-to-end as a single verdict for this artefact; the per-file
      verdicts in \S\ref{sec:verified} were each obtained
      individually.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Discussion and outlook}
\label{sec:discussion}

This paper is a verification artefact: it reports what
\texttt{hexa verify} returned for an enumerated set of claims, with
no LLM self-judgment in the verdict surface. The most important
follow-up is closing the \textsc{Pending} T4 row --- live empirical
contact between the $n=6$ lattice predictions and measured
HumanEval+/hexa-eval scaling curves. Until that lands, the headline
modelling claims remain explicitly $\textsc{Speculation-Fenced}$.
