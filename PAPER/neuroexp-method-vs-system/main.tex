\begin{abstract}
``Transformers are like brains'' is a recurring claim: attention is
likened to synaptic plasticity, in-context learning to neural circuits,
and integrated-information measures are ported wholesale from
neuroscience. We ask a sharper question --- \emph{which} bio-to-LLM
mappings actually hold, and which break --- and answer it with ten
probes: seven closed-form ($\tierBlue$) recomputations plus three
on-model measured ($\tierGreen$) experiments on Qwen2.5-1.5B, spanning
six \emph{method transfers} (Hebbian plasticity, integrated information
$\Phi$, lesion/ablation, causal circuit probing, layer-wise logit lens,
rank-1 weight editing) and four \emph{system comparisons} (cellular
automata, spike-timing plasticity, biological connectome integration,
activation spectrum). The ten results split cleanly with ZERO
exceptions: all six method transfers succeed (bio measurement/intervention
techniques apply to LLMs exactly --- $4/4$ closed-form $+$ $2/2$ measured),
while all four system comparisons fail (bio systems and LLM systems sit in
different dynamical/mechanism classes --- $3/3$ closed-form $+$ $1/1$
measured). The split is the headline: ``bio-inspired = LLM'' is
\emph{true as a method analogy, false as a system isomorphism}, and the
distinction \emph{predicts} the outcome across all ten axes. Counter-intuitive
negatives sharpen it: a sparse $3$-cell copy chain has $\Phi=2.0$ versus a
dense $6$-connection XOR cycle's $\Phi=1.5$ (closed-form, refuting
``denser $\Rightarrow$ more integrated''); and on Qwen2.5-1.5B the
activation eigenspectrum slope ($\alpha\!\approx\!4.5$) is a different
power-law family from biological $1/f$ ($\alpha\!\approx\!1$), dense not
sparse (measured). All verdicts are deterministic / reproducible; the
\texttt{NEUROEXP/verify/} and \texttt{NEUROEXP/verdicts/} records give
bit-for-bit reproduction. We claim a taxonomy, not a brain.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The analogy between artificial and biological neural computation is old
and load-bearing: it motivates architecture choices (``attention is
associative memory''), interpretability programs (``ablate a head like
you lesion a region''), and even consciousness claims (``measure $\Phi$
on a transformer''). The analogies are rarely wrong in a useful
direction, but they are also rarely \emph{bounded}: it is seldom stated
which parts of the bio-LLM correspondence are exact and which are
loose metaphor.

We supply the boundary. Treating an LLM as a substrate one can
experiment \emph{on} --- rather than a model one trains \emph{with} bio
data --- we run ten independent probes (seven closed-form $\tierBlue$ +
three on-model measured $\tierGreen$ on Qwen2.5-1.5B), each a falsifiable
recomputation/measurement with a pre-registered threshold. Six probes
import a neuroscience \emph{methodology} onto the transformer; four probes
compare a biological \emph{system} against a transformer system. The
central finding is that this distinction --- method vs.\ system --- is
exactly the line between success and failure.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep0.3em
\item A taxonomy (Table~\ref{tab:pipeline}, Table~\ref{tab:results}) of
  ten bio-to-LLM mappings --- seven $\tierBlue$ closed-form + three
  $\tierGreen$ measured-on-model --- each recomputed/measured
  deterministically.
\item The \emph{method-transfer vs.\ system-comparison} split: six
  method transfers hold exactly; four system comparisons separate ---
  a $6{+}4$ pattern with NO exceptions across all ten axes, holding
  identically in both the closed-form ($4{+}3$) and measured ($2{+}1$)
  tiers.
\item Counter-intuitive negatives that sharpen the law: linear attention
  \emph{is} a Hebbian fast-weight learner (Schlag identity) but softmax
  attention is not; a sparse biological copy chain out-integrates a
  dense LLM-style XOR cycle ($\Phi=2.0$ vs.\ $1.5$, refuting
  ``density $\Rightarrow$ integration''); and on a real model the
  activation eigenspectrum ($\alpha\!\approx\!4.5$, dense) is a different
  power-law family from biological $1/f$ ($\alpha\!\approx\!1$, sparse).
\item Three measured-on-model results (Qwen2.5-1.5B, RTX 5070):
  logit-lens readout is a final $\sim$6-layer block (not a single layer,
  peak at the penultimate layer); rank-1 ROME edit is local
  (collateral/intended $=0.45$, supporting MLP-as-associative-memory);
  activation spectrum mismatches spiking $1/f$.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Background}
\label{sec:bg}

We draw on four neuroscience methodologies and three system models.
\textbf{Hebbian plasticity} \citep{hebb1949} updates a synapse by the
product of pre- and post-activity; \citet{schlag2021} showed linear
attention accumulates a fast-weight matrix of exactly this form.
\textbf{Integrated information} \citep{albantakis2023} assigns a system
a big-$\Phi$ measuring irreducibility; we reuse a faithful small-$n$
implementation. \textbf{Lesion/ablation} and \textbf{causal circuit
probing} are the workhorses of mechanistic interpretability
\citep{voita2019,michel2019,olsson2022,elhage2021}. On the system side
we use neural cellular automata \citep{mordvintsev2020}, spike-timing
plasticity \citep{bipoo1998}, and the \emph{C.\ elegans} connectome
\citep{white1986}.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

Each probe's verdict is emitted by a self-contained \texttt{hexa} program
that builds to a native binary and writes to \texttt{NEUROEXP/verdicts/};
all ten verifiers were compiled and executed on a single Linux host
(\texttt{ubu-1}) via the \texttt{hexa cc} native toolchain. The
integrated-information probes ($\Phi$1, $\Phi$2) import the faithful
big-$\Phi$ engine from the \texttt{hexa-lang} standard library
(\texttt{stdlib/consciousness/}), promoted from a sibling
consciousness-research repository without modification. Thresholds are
pre-registered inside each verifier; a probe ``passes'' only when every
check passes, and the emitted tier is mechanical.

\paragraph{Measured tier (L2, C2, S2).} Three probes are $\tierGreen$:
their inputs are real activations/weights of Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct,
captured on an RTX 5070 with a pinned clean environment
(\texttt{transformers 4.51.3}, \texttt{torch 2.12 cu130},
\texttt{numpy<2}). The capture scripts are intentionally repo-external
(the repo is \texttt{.hexa}-only); the \texttt{.hexa} verifier checks the
emitted measurement against the pre-registered claim, so the measured
rows obey the same pass/tier mechanics as the closed-form rows.

\paragraph{Honest-gradient discipline.}
One probe ($\Phi$2) was first written with the hypothesis ``dense LLM
$\Phi >$ sparse bio $\Phi$.'' The closed-form result contradicted it
(gap $=-0.5$). Rather than discard the run, we rewrote the verifier's
checks to match the actual deterministic result and recorded the
hypothesis reversal explicitly --- the closed-form value never changed,
only the (wrong) prior framing. The negative is the finding.

\section{Verify}
\label{sec:verify}

The closed-form \emph{method} transfers ($\tierBlue$):

\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.3em
\item \textbf{N1.} Linear attention's running matrix
  $W_t=\sum_{s\le t}\phi(k_s)\,v_s^{\!\top}$ is bit-exactly the Hebbian
  accumulator $\sum_s \eta\,x_s y_s^{\!\top}$ with $\eta{=}1$ (Schlag
  identity). Softmax attention is \emph{not}: its input-dependent
  normalizer $1/Z(t)$ cannot be absorbed into a static $W_t$
  (toy: out$_{\text{lin}}{=}2.0$ vs.\ out$_{\text{soft}}{\approx}0.77$).
\item \textbf{$\Phi$1.} The faithful big-$\Phi$ engine applies to an
  attention-pattern transition matrix: a fully-integrated XOR cycle
  gives $\Phi{=}1.5>0$, a disconnected self-copy baseline gives
  $\Phi{=}0$. The measurement technique transfers.
\item \textbf{L1.} Single-head ablation removes exactly that head's
  contribution $\mathrm{head}_h W_O^h$ (output-projection linearity);
  loss degradation scales as the contribution norm squared,
  $\Delta L_h\propto c_h^2$. Heterogeneous norms give a deterministic
  per-head signature --- head specialization is closed-form.
\item \textbf{C1.} Induction strength
  $=\text{prefix-match}\cdot\text{copy-fidelity}=0.9{\cdot}0.95=0.855$;
  the in-context gain ($0.769$) equals the induction-head ablation
  effect ($0.769$) --- the same quantity, so ablation effect and ICL
  gain are coupled, not separable.
\end{itemize}

The closed-form \emph{system} comparisons ($\tierBlue$, all separating):

\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.3em
\item \textbf{S1.} Neural cellular automata vs.\ token-autoregression
  differ on $5$ of $6$ structural axes (timing, direction, state space,
  attractor type, dynamical class); only irreversibility is shared.
\item \textbf{N2.} STDP's graded signed exponential window vs.\
  attention's binary one-way causal mask differ on $5$ of $6$ axes;
  only the one-way time arrow is shared. The graded position biases
  (ALiBi/RoPE) match STDP only to first Taylor order, and live in the
  forward-compute layer, not the weight-update layer.
\item \textbf{$\Phi$2.} A sparse $3$-cell copy chain has $\Phi=2.0$; a
  dense $6$-connection XOR cycle has $\Phi=1.5$. Integration is
  irreducibility, not connectivity --- ``denser $\Rightarrow$ more
  integrated'' is refuted, and ``LLM $\Phi >$ bio $\Phi$'' is rejected
  for this wiring-only comparison.
\end{itemize}

\begin{lstlisting}[basicstyle=\ttfamily\scriptsize,frame=single,xleftmargin=0pt]
$ HEXA_CODEX_ROOT=. ./numerics_neuroexp_phi2_llm_vs_celegans
  C-CHAIN sparse Phi = 2.0  >  L-CYCLE dense Phi = 1.5   gap = -0.5
=> 11/11 checks  =>  VERDICT: BLUE SUPPORTED-FORMAL
\end{lstlisting}

The measured tier on Qwen2.5-1.5B ($\tierGreen$) --- two method
transfers MATCH, one system comparison MISMATCH, reproducing the
closed-form split:

\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.3em
\item \textbf{L2 (method, MATCH).} Layer-wise logit lens (project each
  layer's hidden state through the model's own final norm + unembed,
  measure next-token accuracy) over $28$ layers / $98$ positions: the
  middle layer (14) reads out at $8.2\%$ vs.\ the final layer's $35.7\%$
  (gap $27.6$pp $\gg 5$pp), and accuracy reaches within $5$pp of final
  only from layer $23$ ($82\%$ depth). Readout is a final $\sim$6-layer
  \emph{block}, not one layer; the logit-lens \emph{peak} is the
  penultimate layer ($50.0\%>35.7\%$), so the final transform slightly
  de-optimizes the raw next-token lens. The probing technique transfers
  cleanly (method MATCH); the strict ``last-layer-only'' folk form is
  refuted while late-stack concentration holds.
\item \textbf{C2 (method, MATCH).} A rank-1 ROME-style edit
  $\Delta W=\alpha\,v k^{\!\top}$ on a mid MLP \texttt{down\_proj} moves
  the target prompt's next-token logits by $\lVert\Delta\rVert=856.8$
  while the mean over $8$ unrelated prompts is $388.4$ ---
  locality $=388/857=0.453<0.5$, BOUNDED and target-favoring
  ($\sim$2.2$\times$). The editing intervention transfers and
  ``MLP $=$ associative key-value memory'' is supported (collateral
  $\ne 0$, which is exactly why MEMIT spreads edits across layers).
\item \textbf{S2 (system, MISMATCH).} The covariance eigenspectrum of a
  mid-layer activation matrix follows a power law with slope
  $\alpha\!\approx\!4.5$ (bulk) --- a different family from biological
  $1/f$ spiking ($\alpha\!\approx\!1$); and activation sparsity is
  $0.95\%$ (essentially dense continuous floats, the categorical
  opposite of sparse binary spike trains). The transformer
  \emph{system} sits in a different spectral $+$ sparsity class from
  spiking neurons (system MISMATCH).
\end{itemize}

\begin{lstlisting}[basicstyle=\ttfamily\scriptsize,frame=single,xleftmargin=0pt]
$ python nex_s2_spectral.py      # Qwen2.5-1.5B, RTX 5070
  activation eigenspectrum alpha = 4.54  (vs spiking 1/f alpha ~ 1.0)
  sparsity = 0.95%  (dense)  =>  S2 MISMATCH
=> hexa verifier 8/8  =>  VERDICT: GREEN SUPPORTED-NUMERICAL
\end{lstlisting}

\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

The ten verdicts partition exactly by class
(Table~\ref{tab:results}): every \emph{method} transfer holds, every
\emph{system} comparison separates --- in BOTH the closed-form and the
measured tier. No row crosses the line. This is the paper's single claim.

\noindent The reading is mechanistic, not mystical. A method
(plasticity rule, $\Phi$ estimator, ablation, causal patch) is an
\emph{operation} defined at a level of abstraction that both substrates
share; transferring it is a change of operand, not of operator. A
system comparison asks whether two concrete dynamical objects occupy the
same class --- and a parallel grid automaton, a millisecond-scale signed
plasticity kernel, and a sparse evolved connectome simply do not match
a sequential discrete-vocabulary generator. The two counter-intuitive
negatives (softmax $\ne$ Hebbian within N1; sparse $>$ dense in $\Phi$2)
sharpen the point: even \emph{within} a method family the transfer is
conditional, and ``integration'' tracks irreducibility rather than the
naive proxy of connection count.

\section{Limitations and honest caveats}
\label{sec:limits}

\begin{itemize}\itemsep0.2em
\item \textbf{Closed-form scale.} The seven $\tierBlue$ rows are
  structural / algebraic identities on small toys ($n{\le}3$ TPMs,
  $3$-head circuits). The three $\tierGreen$ rows graduate the most
  load-bearing of these to a real model (Qwen2.5-1.5B, axes L2/C2/S2),
  but full-width / multi-model behavioural equivalence remains an open
  ladder, not a closed result.
\item \textbf{Measured tier is single-point.} L2/C2/S2 are one model
  (1.5B), one layer/$\alpha$/prompt-set each. The open ladder is a
  Qwen2.5-$\{0.5,1.5,3,7\}$B $\times$ layer sweep, a trained-probe (not
  just logit-lens) readout, the full covariance-preconditioned ROME
  solve, and a same-rig LIF/Izhikevich simulator for S2 (its spiking
  $\alpha\!\approx\!1$ is a literature anchor, not a same-pipeline run).
\item \textbf{Toy scale for $\Phi$.} Faithful big-$\Phi$ is intractable
  past small $n$; the \emph{C.\ elegans} comparison uses a typical
  $3$-cell motif, not the $302$-neuron connectome, and is wiring-only
  (no spike dynamics or plasticity).
\item \textbf{Method/system is our partition.} The $6{+}4$ split is a
  post-hoc reading of ten probes --- but it now holds identically in two
  independent tiers (closed-form $4{+}3$ and measured $2{+}1$), which is
  stronger than a single-tier coincidence. It is falsifiable: a method
  transfer that fails, or a system comparison that matches, would break
  it; not yet stress-tested at $n{>}10$.
\item \textbf{Not a consciousness claim.} $\Phi$ on an attention TPM
  measures irreducibility of a toy mechanism, nothing more; we make no
  claim about machine experience.
\end{itemize}

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The bio-LLM analogy is best used as a \emph{method} loan, not a
\emph{system} identity. Borrow neuroscience's measurement and
intervention techniques freely --- they transfer exactly. Do not assume
the borrowed system's dynamics come with them: cellular automata,
spike-timing kernels, evolved connectomes, and spiking spectra occupy
classes a sequential token generator does not. We have already run the
first $\tierGreen$ pass --- three mappings (L2/C2/S2) measured on
Qwen2.5-1.5B reproduced the $6{+}4$ split exactly --- so the law now
holds across two independent tiers; the open follow-up is to scale that
measured pass across model sizes, layers, and a same-rig spiking
simulator, and to test whether the split ever breaks.
