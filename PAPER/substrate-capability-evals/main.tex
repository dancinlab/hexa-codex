\section{Formula}
\label{sec:formula}
\textcolor{orange}{\textbf{INSUFFICIENT}} — the substrate's measured
Stage-2 best-per-stratum mean-accuracy slope over the
\(0.5\text{B} \to 1.5\text{B} \to 3\text{B} \to 7\text{B}\) Qwen 2.5
parameter ladder fits an empirical exponent of
\(\hat{\sigma} = 0.172\) (log-log OLS, verdict
\texttt{.verdicts/sandbox/f\_codex\_1\_lattice\_lifted.txt}). The n=6
lattice prediction \(N^{24/25} = N^{0.96}\) is 5.6\(\times\) steeper
than the measured slope. Per LATTICE\_POLICY \S 4 ``External envelope''
(see \texttt{LATTICE\_POLICY.md}), the lattice number is OUR
framing for an external substrate (Qwen 2.5 trained without lattice
anchor); it is disclosed but never asserted as a hard residual gate.
Conclusion: no closed-form law currently fits this substrate's accuracy
slope at \(\varepsilon = 0.10\); the cycle-14 saturation finding showed
the underlying curve is \emph{stepwise} (a sharp cliff at wc\_31\_60
between 3B and 7B), so any single-exponent fit is structurally wrong.
A piecewise or sigmoid family is the obvious next functional form.
This section stays \(\bigcirc\) INSUFFICIENT and \emph{is the sole gate}
blocking \texttt{cx\_paper\_gate} for this paper.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
\textcolor{green!50!black}{\textbf{SUPPORTED-NUMERICAL}} — measurement
substrate is a self-hosted M3 Metal mac mini running
\texttt{llama-server} (Homebrew \texttt{llama.cpp} HEAD)
dispatching open-weights Qwen 2.5 GGUF base models
through \texttt{lm\_foundry/tool/route\_dispatch.hexa} (commit
\texttt{b376590}). Scale ladder fully on disk: 0.5B (\texttt{cycle-8 bfd2885}),
1.5B (\texttt{cycle-8 008482e}), 3B (\texttt{cycle-10 56aae56}), 7B
(\texttt{cycle-12 dc9f4fa}). Determinism: cycle-4 \texttt{d\_kv\_prefix\_share}
established bit-identical cache replay at \texttt{top-k=1 / temp=0 /
seed-pinned}; cycle-5 \texttt{395936e} fixed the
\texttt{byte\_exact\_subset} scorer's stderr-trailer-strip bug, eliminating
the \(\sim\)3 pp accuracy inflation on Stage 0 (verdict file
\texttt{.verdicts/sandbox/stage2\_persona\_scaled\_summary.txt} header).
3-persona token-budget grid (\texttt{nano} 32 tok, \texttt{mid} 256 tok,
\texttt{max} 1024 tok); 5 word-count strata
(\texttt{wc\_5\_15, wc\_16\_30, wc\_31\_60, wc\_61\_100, wc\_101\_200});
30 tasks per stratum sampled at stride 13 from the N=2000 manifest.
Total per rung: \(150 \times 3 = 450\) bench rows. All 4 rungs ran at
\$0 incremental on M3 Metal (\(\sim\)66 min total wall-clock).
Cross-link: \texttt{SUBSTRATE.log.md} 2026-05-24 entry (commit
\texttt{37f745c}).

\section{Benchmark}
\label{sec:benchmark}
\textcolor{green!50!black}{\textbf{SUPPORTED-NUMERICAL}} — Stage 2 N=2000
stratified manifest (cycle-5 \texttt{d\_stage2\_scale\_manifest} confirmed,
commit \texttt{91ac831}), 5 word-count strata (\texttt{wc\_5\_15} ..
\texttt{wc\_101\_200}, deterministic generator,

verdict \texttt{m3\_substrate\_saturation\_summary.txt}). Headline

\section{Benefit}
\label{sec:benefit}
\textcolor{green!50!black}{\textbf{SUPPORTED-NUMERICAL}} — the 4-rung
ladder lands two paper-grade benefit deltas:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Stepwise cliff is paper-grade.} The wc\_31\_60 cliff
    jumps \(+43\) pp between 3B and 7B but stays flat (\(\pm 3\) pp)
    between 0.5B and 3B. Single-exponent scaling laws structurally fail
    on stepwise curves (\S Formula). For practitioners choosing a model
    size, the substrate provides a clean ``sub-7B uniformly fails the
    middle stratum on this manifest'' result with \$0 measurement cost.
  \item \textbf{Routing-window is narrow.} \texttt{routing\_simulation\_viable}
    inverts \(\texttt{false} \rightarrow \texttt{true} \rightarrow \texttt{false}\)
    across \(\{0.5\text{B}, 1.5\text{B/3B}, 7\text{B}\}\). At 0.5B all
    personas fail uniformly (\texttt{spread} \(=4\)); at 1.5B--3B the
    cheap-persona-on-easy-task arbitrage opens (\texttt{spread} \(=8\));
    at 7B the cheapest persona already saturates the manifest and the
    spread collapses to 2. Routing-economics papers (cf.\
    \texttt{PAPER/economics-routing-savings/}) must condition their
    benefit claims on this mid-scale window.
\end{itemize}

Both benefit findings are measured with \$0 incremental cost per rung
(self-hosted M3 Metal); the substrate itself is the contribution as
much as the findings.
