  F16) carries the SAME ternary weights, just in a fatter binary container.
  The F16 measurement is empirically foregone: identical weights → identical
  logits → identical scoring. Per g0 Occam, the 5th measurement is redundant —
  the 4 measured rungs + the source-code identity together fix F16 at 6/20

§ Substantive M3 finding (the load-bearing reframing)
  "M3 Pareto" was framed as "is 30% a ternary FLOOR or the 2B4T model CEILING?",
  implicitly assuming quantization precision and storage precision are the same
  axis. They are NOT for BitNet b1.58. The model was PRETRAINED ternary; the
  convert path preserves that regardless of --outtype. So this ladder is a
  STORAGE-precision ladder (file-format bpw, varying 1.5→16), not a QUANTIZATION-
  precision ladder (information-content bpw, fixed at ~1.58 for all 5 rungs).
  All 5 rungs encode the SAME ternary weights — they are the SAME quantization
  at different storage costs. The floor-vs-ceiling distinction is malformed for
  THIS experiment; the substrate-measured fact is "BitNet b1.58 2B4T saturates
  at 6/20 = 30% on the canonical 20-task manifest, regardless of GGUF storage
  container." That stands as the M3 verdict.

§ Cross-confirmation chain
  Combined with M1.5 (ROOT-A: scorer artifact ≤ +5pp) and M2+ (ROOT-B: i2_s ≡
  TQ2_0), 30% is now robust across THREE independent axes:
    (i)   scorer formulation         — byte_exact vs lenient vs answer-extract
    (ii)  ternary-kernel choice      — TQ2_0 (llama.cpp) vs i2_s (bitnet.cpp)
    (iii) GGUF storage container     — i2_s / TQ2_0 / Q4_K_M / Q8_0 / F16(deduced)
  Joint claim "1.58-bit ≈ full quality at a fraction of memory" remains
  FALSIFIED at the MODEL level — within BitNet's own ternarization, no
  storage container recovers the gap.

  Honest scope: "is ternary the bottleneck?" requires a NON-ternary baseline of
  the SAME 2B architecture (e.g. a non-BitNet 2B trained at fp16 from scratch
  on the same data), which this ladder does NOT measure. The Qwen-1.5B-Q4
  baseline that scores 80% on this manifest is a different model family.
