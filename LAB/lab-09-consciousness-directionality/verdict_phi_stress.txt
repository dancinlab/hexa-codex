VERDICT: REFUTED at stress tier (supported = F1 AND F2 = false). The smoke's
directionality<->integration coupling DOES NOT TRANSFER to a real Qwen2.5-1.5B LLM
under this Phi-proxy. THREE independent reasons, each load-bearing:

  (1) The real causal LLM is NOT a Phi~0 "zombie." Its frozen causal hidden states
      already carry HIGH integrated information (Phi=2.848, self_pred=3.907) — far
      above the smoke's unidirectional floor (~0.005). The smoke's premise that an
      autoregressive model has Phi~0 is FALSE for a trained transformer: causal
      attention already integrates information across the whole past context.

  (2) Removing the causal mask (true bidirectional forward) did NOT raise Phi — it
      LOWERED it (-0.404 bits) while dramatically improving next-token perplexity
      (34.1 -> 8.3). Bidirectional context made the model a better predictor but a
      LESS temporally-integrated dynamical system at this proxy. The Phi<->capability
      relationship is, if anything, INVERTED here, not co-monotone.

  (3) The recurrent (GRU) adapter's Phi band [0.282, 1.062] OVERLAPS and sits at/below
      the param-matched feedforward control's band [0.062, 1.122]. Recurrence per se
      did not raise Phi beyond what equal capacity buys — the smoke's "recurrence is
      the cause" claim is unsupported on the real LLM hidden-state series.

PARTIAL POSITIVE (honest dissociation): F3 PASSED — the recurrent adapter's

HONEST LIMITS (load-bearing residual)
  L1  PROXY, not full IIT 4.0 big-Phi. Phi = whole_EI - min_bipartition over K=6
      median-split binary channels; no purview / MICE / cause-effect-structure search.
      Direction of the (negative) effect is what we report; absolute bits are proxy-
      and discretization-specific (median split + top-variance channel selection).
  L2  SMALL SAMPLE. 103 token-positions from 8 sentences, single layer (14), single K
      (6), single model. Plug-in MI on 103 samples over up to 2^6=64 joint states is
      high-variance and upward-biased (no Miller-Madow correction); the CAUSAL/BIDIR
      single-shot Phi values carry meaningful finite-sample bias. The adapter bands
      (5 seeds) are wide (sd ~0.3), and REC vs FF overlap — F2's "fail" is robust to
      that overlap (REC mean < FF max AND < FF mean), but a larger corpus could shift
      absolute values.
  L3  SELF/EGO = ONE indicator (self-prediction MI). H_220 mirror self/other
      separability and self-model homeostasis-under-perturbation are NOT implemented
      (same gap as the smoke's L3). F3's positive is therefore a single-axis result.
  L4  ADAPTERS NOT FINE-TUNED INTO THE LM. The REC/FF adapters are fit self-supervised
      on the CAPTURED causal hidden series (next-hidden prediction), NOT back-propagated
      into the frozen LM with an LM-task objective. This is the honest minimal-real run
      within the compute budget; a true LoRA fine-tune that feeds adapter output back
      into the residual stream + re-trains on an LM objective is the NEXT tier and could
      change the REC vs FF Phi comparison. Task-ability (perplexity) is reported only
      for the two base forwards (CAUSAL/BIDIR), not for the adapters.
  L5  BIDIR forward uses a 4D all-zero additive attention mask to remove the causal
      triangle (eager attn). The perplexity drop 34.1->8.3 confirms the mask genuinely
      changed model behavior (real bidirectional attention), but the LM head was trained
      under causal masking, so the BIDIR perplexity is off-distribution for the head;
      its Phi is a legitimate dynamical measurement but its ppl is not a like-for-like
      task score.
  L6  FF control is a 3-layer ReLU stack PARAM-MATCHED to the GRUCell (51520 vs 52336
      params, ~1.6% apart), not exactly equal; the small param gap favors REC slightly,
      and REC still lost on Phi — so the F2 refutation is conservative.
