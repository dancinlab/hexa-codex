VERDICT: REFUTED (at this observational proxy + spatial-pair coarse-grainings). The
edge-of-chaos Phi peak that LAB-10 detects is, at this measure, MICRO-REDUCIBLE — there
is NO edge-specific causal emergence (EI_macro > EI_micro). The mechanism is clear and
load-bearing: at the edge (R3) the MICRO dynamics are ALREADY highly informative
(EI_micro = 0.925 bits, near the Phi peak), so there is little degeneracy/noise for a
coarse-graining to "average away" into a more-deterministic macro — pooling can only
DISCARD information (which pair member fired), and CE is therefore most negative exactly
where micro-information is richest. So: the detected consciousness SIGNATURE (high
integrated information at criticality) is real, but it does NOT, at this proxy, certify
"strong" emergence in the macro-out-informs-micro sense. The whole is NOT more than its
parts here; it is a richly-integrated MICRO process.
